Contact

I’m glad to hear from students, educators, community members, and researchers at all career stages who share interests in evolutionary biology and ecology. My interests include local adaptation, phenotypic plasticity, and organismal responses to thermal and other environmental stressors, as well as inclusive STEM teaching and outreach.

Students (undergraduate or graduate): I do not run a lab but I am happy to talk about research interests, graduate school pathways, and ways to get involved in research. Reach out with your interests and background!

Collaborators & community partners: I love fieldwork and I also genuinely enjoy data crunching, so I’m especially excited about collaborations that pair field-based research with strong quantitative analysis (physiology + genetics + conservation).

Public & K-12 engagement: I’m interested in developing socially aware science curriculum, especially focused on the mathematics or data science. These ideas are early, and I’d love to build them with educators and community partners.

For questions, shared research interests, or potential connections, contact me at earguetaherr@umass.edu.
